THEIR COURAGE, OUR HISTORY

World War 1 Living Guard of Honour & Memorial – Indian & Canadian Brothers In Arms

Vision

To commemorate the valour of Indian and Canadian Brothers In Arms, to inspire future generations to uphold values of humanity and freedom.

Mission

This memorial highlights the forgotten story of the collaboration between the Indian & Canadian soldiers who fought side by side, at the Western Front, in Europe.

Bring forward shared personal experiences of battle, challenges, emotions and triumph.

To reflect on the lessons from the stories of heroism, sacrifices and voices of liberation.

Project Inception

  • Initial research and documentation of Indian-Canadian military collaboration.
  • Gathering historical records and veteran testimonials.
  • Formation of the memorial committee.

Design Phase

  • Architectural planning and memorial site selection.
  • Artistic collaborations with Canadian and Indian designers.
  • Public consultations and stakeholder engagement.

Fundraising

  • Launch of major donation campaigns in both countries.
  • Corporate sponsorships and government grants secured.
  • Community events raising awareness and support.

Construction

  • Groundbreaking ceremony with dignitaries from both nations.
  • Installation of memorial structures and landscaping.
  • Creation of educational exhibits and interactive displays.

Educational Programs

  • Development of school curriculum materials.
  • Virtual reality historical experiences.
  • Scholar exchange programs between Canadian and Indian institutions.

Grand Opening

  • Inauguration ceremony with military honors from both nations.
  • Annual commemoration events established.
  • Online archives and digital memorial launched worldwide.
