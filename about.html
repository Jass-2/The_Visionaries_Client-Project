About Us

Learn about our mission, vision, and the importance of the memorial

BROTHERS IN ARMS BY

Srishti Foundation

The Brothers In Arms Memorial is more than just a monument—it is a tribute to the bravery, sacrifice, and unity of Indian and Canadian soldiers who fought side by side during World War I. Our mission is to ensure that their stories are never forgotten and that their legacy continues to inspire generations to come. This memorial stands as a symbol of courage, resilience, and the unbreakable bond between two nations.

Vision

To commemorate the valour of Indian and Canadian Brothers In Arms, to inspire future generations to uphold values of humanity and freedom.

Mission

This memorial highlights the forgotten story of the collaboration between the Indian & Canadian soldiers who fought side by side, at the Western Front, in Europe.

Bring forward shared personal experiences of battle, challenges, emotions and triumph.

To reflect on the lessons from the stories of heroism, sacrifices and voices of liberation.

A Monument to Brotherhood and Sacrifice

Over a century ago, Indian and Canadian soldiers fought together, forging a bond through war. Their contributions were immense but often overlooked in mainstream history. This memorial ensures their sacrifices are remembered and honored forever.

What the Memorial Represents

•

A stone monument depicting key battle scenes.

•

Engraved names of fallen soldiers to give them a place of remembrance.

•

A place for education and reflection for future generations.
