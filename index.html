Vision

To commemorate the valour of Indian and Canadian Brothers In Arms, to inspire future generations to uphold values of humanity and freedom.

Mission

This memorial highlights the forgotten story of the collaboration between the Indian & Canadian soldiers who fought side by side, at the Western Front, in Europe.

Bring forward shared personal experiences of battle, challenges, emotions and triumph.

To reflect on the lessons from the stories of heroism, sacrifices and voices of liberation.

Who we are?

Over 135,000 Indian soldiers from the 1.3 million-strong Indian Army Corps fought alongside Canadian soldiers in historic battles such as Ypres, St. Julian, Festubert, The Somme, and Vimy.

Discover the untold story of Indian and Canadian soldiers who stood together on the Western Front. This video explores their bravery, sacrifices, and the significance of the "Living Guard of Honour & Memorial"—a tribute to their legacy and the shared history of valor.

Preserving History, Inspiring Generations

Our mission is to honor the forgotten bravery of Indian and Canadian soldiers who stood side by side during World War I. Through this initiative, we aim to educate future generations about their sacrifices and ensure their legacy is never forgotten.

A Tribute to Brotherhood & Valor

The Living Guard of Honour & Memorial serves as a lasting tribute to the courage, unity, and resilience of those who fought together. It is a symbol of remembrance, fostering awareness and appreciation for their shared history.

Your support helps us maintain the memorial and continue our important work in the community. Every contribution counts towards preserving our shared legacy.
