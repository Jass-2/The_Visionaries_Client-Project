THEIR COURAGE, OUR HISTORY

Honoring the Past,
Building for the Future

The Brothers in Arms Memorial is more than a monument—it's a tribute to the courage, sacrifices, and brotherhood of the Indian and Canadian soldiers who stood together in World War I. Through our initiatives, we strive to ensure their stories are remembered, their families supported, and their legacy preserved for future generations.

A Legacy in Stone

THE BROTHERS IN ARMS MEMORIAL

More than a monument, the Brothers in Arms Memorial stands as a lasting tribute to the brave Indian and Canadian soldiers who fought side by side during WWI. This memorial will serve as a place of remembrance, reflection, and education for generations to come.

Bring This Memorial to Life

Keeping Their Stories Alive

Many people are unaware of the deep historical ties between Indian and Canadian soldiers during WWI. Our awareness campaigns aim to shed light on their sacrifices and ensure their stories are told far and wide.

Educational Programs

Talks (e.g., school assemblies), school outreach.

Social Media & Digital Initiatives

Hashtags, video series, storytelling campaigns.

Events & Commemorations

Preserving Family Legacies

EVERY SOLDIER HAS A STORY

Behind every soldier was a family - parents, siblings, spouses, and children who supported them from afar. Their letters, photographs, and memories form a crucial part of our shared history. We're collecting these precious family stories to ensure they're preserved for future generations.

If your family has a connection to the Indian or Canadian soldiers who served in WWI, we want to hear from you. Your story is an important piece of this historical puzzle.
